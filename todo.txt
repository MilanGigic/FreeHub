Pro-Tip for your Developer Workflow:

Since the NBS (National Bank of Serbia) has a public API/Web Service, you should set up a Cron Job (a scheduled task) that runs every morning at 08:00 AM.

    It fetches the new rates.

    It inserts them into this table.

    That way, when your user opens the app to log a payment, the rate is already there waiting for them.
